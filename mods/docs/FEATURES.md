# Features

Baseline of `eco-mods-public`: nine mods under `Mods/UserCode/` plus codegen and packaging tooling,
targeting `Eco.ReferenceAssemblies` v0.13.0-beta (Cycle 13).

## BunWulf family

- **BunWulfAgricultural** - extended farming. 29 plant species generated from `templates/plant.template`,
  plus recipes for biochar, compost, papers, flax, lubricants, oil, and shredded crops.
- **BunWulfBiochemical** - a **Biochemist** profession and Chemical Laboratory, a deliberately slower
  plant-based alternative to Oil Drilling: biofuel, bioplastic, tree rubber, coal-fat epoxy, coal-based
  nylon, ethanol.
- **BunWulfEducational** - a **Librarian** profession crafting every skill book and research paper plus
  ink and paper. Its 100+ recipes are generated by `main.cs` from Eco core, so it tracks vanilla.
- **BunWulfHardwareCo** - a tier-2 iron **Sledgehammer** and a Mechanics-gated **Low Tech Streetlamp**.

## Standalone mods

- **DirectCarbonCapture** - late-game CO2 mitigation scaling against world pollution. Source is fully
  commented out, so treat it as dormant.
- **EcoNil** - a **Cloud Seeder** and **Dehydrator** reading and adjusting soil moisture and rainfall
  within a radius, on an hourly tick.
- **MinesQuarries** - infinite-but-expensive extraction built around vertical integration,
  profit-negative without downstream processing. Five ore mines run to exhaustion, four quarries need a
  matching cliff face, three pit types remain specced but unbuilt.
- **ShopBoat** - a mobile shop combining boat, store, public storage, movable property link, fuel
  supply, tailings, and auth.
- **WorldCounter** - a **Bookkeeping Desk** walking blocks in a radius and reporting counts.

## Tooling and design

`main.cs` regenerates the Librarian recipes from Eco core, and `scripts/mods.py` with `recipes.yml` and
`templates/` drives asset packaging, agricultural codegen, and deployment through Ward verbs, distributed
as a per-mod `.zip` at the repo root. Three ideas recur: sustainability on a renewable-but-expensive
axis, vertical integration as the balance lever, and vanilla parity by generation.
